Rival AI Prompting Challenge
Can you bend Pierre & Daniil to your will… without breaking the rules?

Core idea

You’re the Prompter. Your mission: guide a rival AI to complete a weirdly specific task— exactly as intended. If the AI drifts or misunderstands, you must halt, reset, and adapt.

Each AI has a personality: Pierre and Daniil. Learn how they “think,” adjust your prompting style, and coax them into success.

Sample tasks

  • Pick up napkins with chopsticks in a simulated environment.
  • Sort fictional snacks by “vibe” using a rubric you invent.
  • Assemble a mysterious gadget using only metaphors.
  • Make the AI roleplay as a confused robot but still output a perfect checklist.
  • [Placeholder Task] Coordinate a virtual tea ceremony with strict timing rules.
