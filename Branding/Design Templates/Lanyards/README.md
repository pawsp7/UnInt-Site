# Lanyards (45 cm × 2 cm, plus 15 mm)

Dye-sublimation strap art. Type sits 2.45 mm inside the cut on the 20 mm masters. Artwork is outlined — shops do not need the fonts.

20 mm files are the default. 15 mm files keep the same 450 mm length, use a `-15mm` suffix, and do not replace the 20 mm masters.

## Club templates (one colour, no names)

Two nameless straps themed around the club, not people. Colourways: **white on ink**, **ink on white**, and **transparent** overlays. Layouts: icon set, repeating lockup, and alternating mascot / name / slogan.

### Icon set

Each file is **450 mm × 20 mm** with three 150 mm beats. Every beat has the official jellyfish (solid fill), **UNNATURAL / INTELLIGENCE**, the dual bar mark, four vector-kit icons, a tagline, and `unnaturalintelligence.club`.

| Beat | Icons | Tagline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | token, prompt, halt, restart | STAY AHEAD |
| 2 | spark, tentacle, mask, neural | GENERATED BY AI |
| 3 | workflow, commit, ticket, node | PERFECTED BY HUMANS |

Icons are the kit marks flattened to a single ink colour. Ticket holes and mask eyes knock out to the strap colour.

## Send to a printer

1. Prefer `png/*-300dpi.png` or the matching print PDF. SVG is the vector master.
2. Size **450 × 20 mm** (17.72 × 0.79 in) or **450 × 15 mm** (17.72 × 0.59 in) at 100%, matching the filename. Do not add extra bleed unless the shop asks — the rails run to the edge.
3. Club files are one colour (RGB white or ink) except `club-colour-repeat`, `club-colour-repeat-wide-rail`, and `club-colour-repeat-15mm`, which are full colour RGB. Transparent lockup/alt PNGs keep alpha. Exec files are full colour RGB. Same art both sides of the strap.
4. If the shop wants a longer loop, they can tile the 150 mm repeat; each file already contains three repeats.

Open `preview.html` before ordering.
