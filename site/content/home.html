What we practice

Three foundations, one room.

Most campus talk about AI is either hype or a ban. We destigmatise it, then get specific about the work.

01

Untapped potential

Find the uses default chat never gets asked. The button nobody opens. The shop-floor photo. The one recurring task that still has no assistant.

02

Workflow integration

Fold AI into Tuesday, not into a demo. Class, practicum, job search, client email, shop estimates — keep only what you still open next week.

03

Lean tokens, human finish

Spend less, then put a human finish on whatever comes back so it still sounds like a person. The mask of humanity is the point, not a disclaimer.
