Start on Rubric.

Membership is handled on our Saitsa Rubric page. That is also where event tickets and club news will show up when we post them.

  1. Open the club on Rubric and sign in with your SAIT email. If you have never used the clubs portal, create the Rubric password it asks for — it is separate from your SAIT login.
  2. Join Unnatural Intelligence. If the page is still sparse, email us and an exec will add you.
  3. Hop into Discord for the week-to-week. Instagram is for the public posters and photos.
  4. Come to a confirmed event. You can show up before you feel like “a member.”
