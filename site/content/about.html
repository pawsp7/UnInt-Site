Four things every student should know

What we will not do

We do not write your graded work, hide that a model was used, or paste confidential SAIT data into a public chatbot. If you could not stand behind the output without the model, it is not finished.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Workshops are built for every program. If a night is technical, the listing will say so.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership is open to all SAIT students. A fee is allowed under Saitsa rules and is not required for us to operate. If we ever charge one, the amount will be on the Rubric page before you pay it.

Can staff or friends from off campus come?

Community members, including SAIT staff and faculty, may join at the execs’ discretion. They cannot hold an executive role or vote. At least three quarters of members have to be current SAIT students.

Where do I officially join?

On our Rubric page: campus.hellorubric.com/?s=16226. Discord is the hangout. Rubric is the membership record Saitsa uses.
