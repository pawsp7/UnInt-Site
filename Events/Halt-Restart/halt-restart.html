Halt & Restart

You are the Prompter. Pierre and Daniil are the AIs. Write hyper-specific instructions. If the model drifts, halt it and start over. Adapt to the AI. The tightest prompt takes the prize.

Model 01 · v0.9-clarifier

Pierre

Will not move until every noun is defined. Be clear, be specific. If you say “it,” he waits for a name.

Daniil

Follows the prompt word for word. Will not infer missing steps. Vague verbs get the most literal reading in the room.
