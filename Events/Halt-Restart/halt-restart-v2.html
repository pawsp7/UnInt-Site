Live prompting lab · Fall 2026 · Purpose: prompt two human AIs

Halt &Restart

You are the Prompter. Pierre and Daniil are the rival AIs. Write hyper-specific instructions. If the model drifts, halt it and start over. Adapt to the AI.

  1. T01

    Move a ping pong ball from Cup A to be inside Cup B

  2. T02

    Pick up a napkin with chopsticks

  3. T03

    Stack 6 items in some way that they stand for 10 seconds

  4. T04

    Sort 5 objects by size, shape, weight, or colour

  5. T05

    Draw a jellyfishGrand prize · $25 gift card

Model 01 · v0.9-clarifier

Pierre

Will not move until every noun is defined. Vague verbs pause the run. If you say “it,” he waits for a name.

Model 02 · v0.9-literal

Daniil

Follows the prompt word for word. Will not infer missing steps. Vague verbs get the most literal reading in the room.

01

Pick a rival

Walk up to Pierre or Daniil. One model per attempt. They only hear the prompt you speak.

02

Write it tight

Name the objects, order, grip, and stop point. Implied common sense does not count.

03

They execute

The AI performs the physical task as prompted. Do not grab the props unless you halt.

04

Halt. Restart.

If it is not doing the task as intended, say HALT. Freeze. Rewrite. Restart.

05

Adapt. Win.

Each model behaves differently. Change your instructions to match.
