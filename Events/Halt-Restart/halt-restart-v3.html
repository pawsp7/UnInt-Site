Halt &Restart

Purpose: a live prompting lab. You are the Prompter. Pierre and Daniil are the rival AIs. Write hyper-specific instructions. If the model drifts, halt and restart. Adapt to the AI.

01

Pick a rival

Walk up to Pierre or Daniil. One model per attempt. They only hear the prompt you speak.

02

Write it tight

Name the objects, order, grip, and stop point. Implied common sense does not count.

03

They execute

The AI performs the physical task as prompted. Do not grab the props unless you halt.

04

Halt. Restart.

If it is not doing the task as intended, say HALT. Freeze. Rewrite. Restart from a clean prompt.

05

Adapt. Win.

Each model behaves differently. Change your instructions to match.

Model 01 · v0.9-eager

Pierre

Starts before you finish the sentence. Fast, cheerful, and likely to complete a nearby task that was not the task.

Model 02 · v0.9-literal

Daniil

Follows the prompt word for word. Will not infer missing steps. Vague verbs get the most literal reading in the room.
