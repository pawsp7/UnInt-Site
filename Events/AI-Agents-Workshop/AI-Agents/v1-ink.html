AI Agents What? How? Use.

Official title · AI Agents: What? How? & Learning to Use Them

Purpose: a friendly two-hour workshop that demystifies AI agents for a non-technical audience. Chatbots answer. Agents act. No prior experience — every attendee leaves having used an AI agent for a real task.

no prior experience required
Loop 01

Perceive

Notice the task, the files, the question. An agent starts by taking in the world around the prompt.

Loop 02

Decide

Pick a next useful step. Unlike a chatbot, it plans — then chooses a tool instead of only talking.

Loop 03

Act

Call a tool, check the result, repeat. Limitations and risks stay on the table the whole time.
