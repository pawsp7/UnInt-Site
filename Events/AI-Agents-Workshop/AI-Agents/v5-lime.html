AI Agents: What? How? & Learning to Use Them

A friendly two-hour workshop for the whole SAIT community. No prior experience. Chatbots answer. Agents act. Every attendee leaves having used an AI agent for a real task.

Part 1 · 30 min

What?

Chatbot vs agent. Everyday examples. Live demos you can interrupt.

Part 2 · 30 min

How?

Perceive → decide → act. Tools, limitations, and the risks of improvising.

Part 3 · 30 min

Use.

You run an agent on studying, a resume, or a project. Then a human finish.
