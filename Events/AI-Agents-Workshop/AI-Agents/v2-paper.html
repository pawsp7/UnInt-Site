AI AgentsWhat? How? Use.

AI Agents: What? How? & Learning to Use Them

A friendly two-hour workshop for the whole SAIT community. No prior experience. Chatbots answer. Agents act. Every attendee leaves having used an AI agent for a real task.

01 · Perceive

See it

Notice the task, the files, the question. An agent starts by taking in more than the chat window.

02 · Decide

Plan it

Pick a next useful step. It chooses a tool instead of only talking back.

03 · Act

Do it

Call a tool, check the result, repeat. Limitations and risks stay on the table.
