## 2. Event description

A friendly, 2-hour workshop that demystifies AI agents for a non-technical audience. The event falls under the club's "Learn" pillar: making AI accessible, practical and social for the whole SAIT community. No prior experience is required — the goal is that every attendee leaves having actually used an AI agent for a real task.

### Agenda (2 hours)

| Duration | Segment |
|---|---|
| 15 min | Welcome, club presentation, new member sign-ups |
| 30 min | Part 1 — What is an AI agent? (chatbot vs agent, everyday examples, live demos) |
| 30 min | Part 2 — How do they work? (perceive → decide → act loop, tools, limitations and risks) |
| 30 min | Part 3 — Hands-on: attendees use an AI agent for a concrete task (studying, resume, project) |
| 15 min | Q&A and open discussion |

## 3. Objectives

- Demystify AI agents for students of all programs — no technical background needed.
- Give every attendee hands-on experience using an AI agent for a real task.
- Recruit new club members.
- Promote the club's "Learn" pillar and feed the pipeline of future events (mini-hackathons, creative demos).
