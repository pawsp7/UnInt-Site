UnnaturalGames

You don’t need to know how to play. You need an opinion. Chess against a language model. The crowd votes every human move.

01

Argue.

Casual players name a move. Stronger players explain why it’s wrong. Three minutes, then we vote.

02

Vote.

Show of hands or a QR poll. The room is a single team. The winning idea is the move we play.

03

Listen.

It moves, then says why. The moderator reads that out loud. That’s the funny part.
