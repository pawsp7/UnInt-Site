Unnatural Games
posters

Three tabloid (11×17) ideas for the social game night. One chessboard, one language model, the room voting as a single team. The shell is the glitch wordmark, gradient jellyfish, staggered bars, and the four-colour jellyfish row. Each sheet also carries the Saitsa facts: when, where, type, purpose, contact, the unaltered Saitsa Clubs logo, and the SAIT wordmark.

Edit date, room, and the shared lines in copy.js, then reload. “Mondays” and “Room TBA” are placeholders. SECC approval covers one night — don’t print a weekly promise until a repeat is actually approved. Fact-bar, grid, and border templates are in templates.html. Fifteen Games logos, five of them built around the jellyfish, are in logos.html.
